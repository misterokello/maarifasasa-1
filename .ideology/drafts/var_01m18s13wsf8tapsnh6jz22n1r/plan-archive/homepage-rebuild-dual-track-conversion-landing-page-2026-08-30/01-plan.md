# Homepage rebuild: dual-track conversion landing page

Rebuild `/` as a modern, dynamic landing page that serves two audiences from one story — learners who want skills and income, and employers/funders/governments who want verified talent — while driving enrolment through live, admin-managed course data.

## What a page like this must do

1. **Say the mission in one breath.** Hero with the positioning from the master deck: "Building Africa's Digital Workforce Engine" and the 1,000,000 earning opportunities by 2030 promise.
2. **Split the audience immediately.** Two clear doors ("I want to learn and earn" / "I'm hiring or partnering") so neither audience has to guess.
3. **Show the pathway, not a course list.** The three moves — Build the skills → Convert to placements → Sustain access & belonging — as an interactive stepper.
4. **Prove it with numbers.** 10,000+ learners trained, ~2,000 verified placements, UGX 1.2B earned annually by our talent, Mastercard Foundation EdTech Fellowship credibility.
5. **Make enrolling the easiest action on the page.** Live featured courses and upcoming cohorts pulled from the products table, each card going straight to `/checkout?product=slug`.
6. **Keep a CTA always reachable.** Sticky mobile CTA bar plus a closing dual-CTA band.
